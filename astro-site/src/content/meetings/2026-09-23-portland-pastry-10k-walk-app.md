Join us fourth Wednesday September 23rd for more of your favorite open source geo-goodness. September's special guest is Regan Hutson who has worked with both Enterprise and Open-Source geospatial tools for over 15 years. This has included incorporating spatial analysis into various roles at Kaiser Permanente, as well as pro-bono work for groups like the Los Angeles Regional Food Bank.

Regan's presentation will outline decision points and development steps that went into building a mobile-focused web-map to support the first ever Portland Pastry 10K Walk event.

As always there will be pizza, and BYOB is available from across the hall at HotLips!
