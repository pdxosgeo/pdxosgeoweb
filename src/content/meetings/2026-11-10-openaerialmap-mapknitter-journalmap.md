Matthew Lippincott will be talking about [OpenAerialMap](https://openaerialmap.org/), [Mapknitter](https://github.com/publiclab/mapknitter), and what a participatory archive of aerial imagery might look like, from the perspective of someone working with community groups on mapping environmental degradation, restoration, and pollution.

Tim Welch will talk fast and scalable geo-search with [Elastic Search](https://www.elastic.co/elasticsearch). Case study - Journalmap [http://journalmap.org](http://journalmap.org), and immersive storytelling with maps - [https://github.com/twelch/reactmap-tour](https://github.com/twelch/reactmap-tour). (Mapbox+React+Electron+QGIS)
