Tim Welch will present Turf.js, a Javascript library for spatial analysis. He'll cover the basics of what Turf is, where it fits in the toolbox, and how you can piece together its many modules to do useful things using the common language of GeoJSON. [https://turfjs.org](https://turfjs.org)
